Stream Hive of Unholy Anthropods

Divine Autopsy is an emerging rock band known for their dark and emotionally intense tone of music. They’re a regional band of young adult men located in Reykjavik, Iceland. With their music they choose to tackle themes related to suffering in different situations. Specifically, themes relating to isolation, paranoia, dissociation, and general internal collapse.
